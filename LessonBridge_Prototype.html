ADAPTED ACTIVITY · GRADE 3
English + Español

Build the Food Web

Collaborative activity · 15–20 minutes · 3–4 students per group

Learning outcome

Students explain how living things depend on one another in an ecosystem and use evidence from the lesson to support their ideas.

Student directions

1. Look at the picture cards. Put them in a food-web order.
2. Point to two cards. Tell your partner how they are connected.
3. Switch roles. Your partner explains a different connection.
4. Choose one connection your group can explain to the class.

Visual routine

👀 Look → 🧩 Connect → 💬 Explain → 🔄 Switch

Language supports

English
“___ depends on ___ because ___.”
“I agree with ___ because…”
Español
“___ depende de ___ porque ___.”
“Estoy de acuerdo con ___ porque…”

Teacher / substitute guide

Say: “Your job is to help everyone explain. Students may point, draw, gesture, or use their home language before trying the English frame.”
Prompt: “What does this organism need? Show me on the cards.”
Look for: Every student contributes at least one idea; students use evidence from the cards or lesson.
